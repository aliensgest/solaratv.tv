Best IPTV Apps for Android TV 2026 — 7 Tested & Ranked

June 3, 2026 SOLARA TV Tech Team 12 min read

Android TV and Google TV (Sony Bravia, TCL, Hisense, Xiaomi Mi Box, Nvidia Shield, Chromecast 4K with Google TV) make excellent IPTV platforms — but only if you install the right player. We spent 6 weeks testing the most popular IPTV apps with a SOLARA TV M3U playlist (25,000+ channels) to find the best ones.

Quick verdict: TiviMate Premium is the best paid app (best UI + EPG). IPTV Smarters Pro is the best free option. OTT Navigator is the best for power users. Install via Aptoide TV, Downloader app, or sideload the APK.

How We Tested

Each app was loaded with the same SOLARA TV M3U + EPG URL and tested on a Nvidia Shield Pro, Chromecast 4K with Google TV, and Xiaomi Mi Box S. We scored on: UI/navigation, EPG quality, channel zapping speed, picture/audio quality, catch-up support, recording, and stability.

The 7 Best IPTV Apps for Android TV 2026

#1 Best Overall

TiviMate IPTV Player

★★★★★ · Free + Premium €25/year · Available on Aptoide TV

The undisputed king of Android TV IPTV. Stunning UI built specifically for D-pad remotes, lightning-fast channel zapping, the best EPG of any IPTV app, multi-playlist support, and built-in recording in the Premium version.

Pros
  • Best-in-class EPG with show details and series catch-up
  • Picture-in-picture, multi-view (up to 4 channels)
  • Native 4K HDR support, Dolby Audio passthrough
  • Smooth navigation, instant channel switch
Cons
  • Full features behind €25/year paywall
  • Not on Google Play Store (sideload required)
#2 Best Free

IPTV Smarters Pro

★★★★½ · 100% free · Available on Google Play Store

The most popular free IPTV app on Android TV. Easy to set up (paste your M3U URL or Xtream Codes login), clean interface, supports VOD, series, EPG, parental controls, and external player integration.

Pros
  • Free with no ads
  • Supports M3U, Xtream Codes API, and EPG
  • VOD + Series + Live in one app
  • External player support (MX Player, VLC)
Cons
  • EPG less polished than TiviMate
  • Channel switching slightly slower
#3 Best for Power Users

OTT Navigator IPTV

★★★★½ · Free + Premium ~€20/year

Deep customization heaven. Multiple themes, advanced EPG, archive/catch-up, time-shift, cloud sync of favorites across devices. Steeper learning curve but rewards tech-savvy users.

Pros
  • Most customization options
  • Excellent catch-up & archive features
  • Cloud sync favorites & settings
Cons
  • Overwhelming for beginners
  • UI looks dated compared to TiviMate
#4

GSE Smart IPTV

★★★★ · Free with ads + Premium IAP

Reliable veteran app that works on Android TV, iOS, and Apple TV — perfect if you want one app across all platforms. Strong on Chromecast support.

Pros
  • Cross-platform (Android, iOS, Apple TV)
  • Chromecast built-in
  • Multiple playlist formats supported
Cons
  • Free version shows ads
  • UI cluttered on Android TV remotes
#5

Sparkle TV (formerly Sparkle TV Pro)

★★★★ · ~€10 one-time

Minimalist player with a focus on speed. Great for older Android TV boxes where TiviMate may lag. One-time payment instead of subscription.

Pros
  • One-time purchase, no subscription
  • Lightweight, runs on low-end hardware
  • Fast channel switching
Cons
  • Fewer features than TiviMate
  • Limited VOD/Series support
#6

XCIPTV Player

★★★½ · Free + premium add-ons

Solid alternative to IPTV Smarters with a different visual style. Supports Xtream Codes, M3U, EPG, parental control, and multiple users.

Pros
  • Multiple user profiles
  • Built-in player + external player support
Cons
  • UI feels slightly cluttered
#7

Perfect Player

★★★ · Free + Premium €5

The original Android IPTV player. Old-school but reliable. Best for people who just want channels + EPG without bells and whistles.

Pros
  • Lightweight & fast
  • Cheap premium unlock
Cons
  • UI looks 2018-era
  • No native Google TV optimization

How to Install IPTV Apps on Android TV (Sideload)

Most of these apps aren't on the Google Play Store for Android TV. Here's how to install them:

  1. Enable unknown apps: Settings » Device Preferences » Security & Restrictions » Unknown sources » allow your browser/Downloader.
  2. Install Downloader app from Google Play Store (yes, it's there).
  3. Open Downloader and enter the app's download URL (e.g., tivimate.com or iptvsmarters.com).
  4. Install the APK » Open » Add your SOLARA TV M3U URL.
Pro tip: For the smoothest experience use Nvidia Shield Pro (most powerful Android TV box) or Chromecast 4K with Google TV (best value at ~€60). Both handle 4K HDR with no buffering.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free IPTV app for Android TV in 2026?
IPTV Smarters Pro is the best free option in 2026 — feature-rich, ad-free, available on Google Play Store, supports M3U and Xtream Codes.
Is TiviMate worth the €25/year?
Yes, especially if you watch 4+ hours/day. The polished UI, fast EPG, recording, and multi-view features make it the best Android TV IPTV experience.
Can I install IPTV apps on Sony Bravia or TCL Google TV?
Yes. Google TV is based on Android TV, so all the apps in this guide work. Use the Downloader method to sideload TiviMate.
Do I need a strong Android TV box?
Any recent Android TV box (2022+) handles 1080p easily. For 4K HDR, use Nvidia Shield Pro, Chromecast 4K with Google TV, or Xiaomi Mi Box S 2nd gen.
Will my IPTV subscription work on multiple Android TV devices?
Yes — SOLARA TV allows simultaneous streams in your household. You can install IPTV Smarters on the living room TV and TiviMate on the bedroom TV using the same M3U URL.
