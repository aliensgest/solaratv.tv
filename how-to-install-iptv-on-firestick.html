Step-by-Step Installation (2026 Updated Method)

1. Unlock Developer Options (The "Hidden" Menu)

Amazon has updated its interface to hide developer settings. To enable them:

  • Navigate to Settings > My Fire TV > About.
  • Select your Fire TV Stick name and click the center button on your remote 7 times.
  • A notification will appear: "No need, you are already a developer."
  • Go back to Developer Options and switch Install Unknown Apps to ON.

2. Install the Downloader App

  • From the Firestick Home Screen, go to the Find (Magnifying Glass) icon.
  • Search for "Downloader" and install the official orange app.
  • Launch Downloader and grant the necessary permissions to access your files.

3. Configure Your SolaraTV Subscription

  • Inside the Downloader app, enter the unique download code for the SolaraTV Premium Player (sent to you immediately after joining).
  • Once downloaded, click Install and then Open.
  • Enter your SolaraTV login credentials.
