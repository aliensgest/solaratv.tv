How to Fix IPTV Buffering on Android TV

Buffering is one of the most common IPTV problems. To improve streaming quality:

  • Use Ethernet instead of WiFi
  • Use 5GHz WiFi connection
  • Restart your Android TV device
  • Clear IPTV app cache regularly
  • Close background apps
  • Use high-speed internet (25+ Mbps for 4K)
  • Update IPTV applications frequently
  • Use a VPN if your ISP throttles IPTV traffic
