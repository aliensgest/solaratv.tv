The IPTV Reseller Model Explained

What is an IPTV credit?

A credit = 1 subscription month for 1 customer. You buy a pack of credits from your wholesale provider (e.g., 60 credits for €135 = €2.25 each). You then sell each credit to your customer as a 1-month, 3-month, 6-month, or 12-month plan at retail price.

Common Reseller Mistakes to Avoid

  • Picking a cheap unreliable provider — customer refunds will eat your margin.
  • Underpricing — €5/month attracts the worst customers (complainers, refund chasers).
  • No free trial — give every prospect a free trial; conversion jumps to 50%+.
  • Slow support — IPTV customers expect WhatsApp replies in minutes.
  • Promising "everything" — only promise what your provider reliably delivers.
