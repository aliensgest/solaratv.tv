How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung, LG & Android Smart TVs (2027 Complete Guide)

Written by the SolaraTV Support Team • Works with any Xtream Codes API or M3U Playlist • Back to Supported Devices

Quick Answer

On Android TV or Google TV (Sony, TCL, Philips, Hisense Google TV models), install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro from the Google Play Store and log in with your SolaraTV Xtream Codes credentials. On Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS), open your TV's built-in app store and install an IPTV player supporting Xtream Codes or M3U (availability varies by TV model year and region). If your TV store lacks a compatible player, connecting an Amazon Firestick or Android TV box is the fastest and most reliable alternative.

What You Need Before You Start

  • Your SolaraTV Login Credentials: Either Xtream Codes API details (Server URL, Username, Password) or an M3U Playlist URL.
  • Stable Internet Connection: Minimum 25 Mbps for Full HD and 50+ Mbps for 4K streaming—ideally connected via an Ethernet cable.
  • Updated TV Firmware: Open your TV's Settings → Support / System → Software Update to ensure the latest OS version is installed.

How to Set Up IPTV on Android TV or Google TV

  1. Open the Google Play Store from your TV's home screen.
  2. Search for and install a reliable IPTV player. TiviMate offers the cleanest traditional TV guide interface, while IPTV Smarters Pro is simple and easy to configure.
  3. Launch the app and select Login with Xtream Codes API (or Add Playlist → Xtream Codes).
  4. Enter your Server URL, Username, and Password exactly as provided by SolaraTV (make sure to include http:// and the port number if included).
  5. Save your profile and wait 1–2 minutes for all live channels, VOD movies, and the TV guide to finish loading.
  6. Open the app's EPG settings and enable Automatic TV Guide Updates.

How to Set Up IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV (Tizen)

  1. Press the Home button on your Samsung remote and open Apps.
  2. Select the Search icon and look for a compatible IPTV player supporting Xtream Codes or M3U (such as Smart IPTV, IBO Player, or DuplexPlay—availability depends on your model year and region).
  3. Check the app description before installing, as some Tizen players require a one-time app activation fee after a free trial period.
  4. Install and open the player. Most Samsung IPTV apps display a Device ID or MAC Address on the first screen.
  5. Visit the player's official activation/playlist portal on your phone or PC, enter your TV's MAC/Device ID, and upload your SolaraTV M3U URL or Xtream Codes. Safety tip: Only enter your playlist on the app's official website.
  6. Restart the IPTV app on your Samsung TV to load your channel list.

If your Samsung TV model does not list a suitable player in the store, skip ahead to the streaming stick option below.

How to Set Up IPTV on an LG Smart TV (webOS)

  1. Press the Home button on your LG Magic Remote and open the LG Content Store (labeled Apps on newer webOS models).
  2. Use the search bar to find an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U playlists.
  3. Install and launch the application, then write down the Device ID / MAC Address shown on screen.
  4. Enter your SolaraTV credentials directly inside the app (if supported) or upload your M3U/Xtream playlist via the player's official web portal.
  5. Close and reopen the app on your LG TV so the live channels and EPG synchronize properly.

When to Use a Firestick or Android TV Box Instead

Even if you own a modern Smart TV, adding a dedicated streaming device is often the superior long-term setup:

  • Better App Selection: Top-tier players like TiviMate are built specifically for Android TV and Fire OS, not Tizen or webOS.
  • Faster Performance: A dedicated streaming stick has a faster processor and more memory than most built-in TV chips, resulting in instant channel switching and smoother 4K playback.
  • Consistent App Updates: Built-in Smart TV stores occasionally remove or delay IPTV app updates, whereas Android/Fire TV apps receive regular improvements.

Tips for Smooth Playback on Any Smart TV

  • Use Wired Ethernet: Almost all Smart TVs have a rear LAN port—using a cable eliminates Wi-Fi interference and prevents buffering.
  • Disable Motion Smoothing for Live Sports: Turn off features like Auto Motion Plus (Samsung) or TruMotion (LG) if fast-moving sports look jittery.
  • Enable Auto EPG Updates: Configure your player to refresh the TV guide automatically every 24 hours.
  • Troubleshoot Buffering Quickly: If streams pause during peak hours, check our IPTV Buffering Fix Guide or message SolaraTV support on WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I watch IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV without a Firestick?

Yes, if your Samsung TV's app store includes a compatible IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U playlists. Availability depends on the TV's model year and country. If no player appears in your store, connecting an Amazon Firestick or Android TV box is the simplest solution.

Which IPTV app is best for LG Smart TVs?

Open the LG Content Store and choose an actively updated player that supports Xtream Codes API or M3U links and has positive user reviews. If your LG model is older or sluggish, pairing it with a Firestick running TiviMate or IPTV Smarters will give you a much smoother experience.

Is TiviMate available on Samsung or LG TVs?

No. TiviMate is developed exclusively for Android TV, Google TV, and Amazon Fire TV devices. On Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS), use a player from your TV's built-in app store or connect an external Android/Fire TV device.

Why does my Smart TV IPTV app ask for a MAC address?

Many Smart TV IPTV players bind your playlist to your TV's unique Device ID or MAC address. Instead of typing a long URL with your TV remote, you upload your SolaraTV M3U or Xtream Codes credentials on the app's official website, and your TV downloads the channels automatically.
